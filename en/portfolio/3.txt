# Real Talka: AI Voice Sales for Enterprises


## Context

- Designed for B2B sales teams that need an AI copilot capable of live presentations, knowledge-grounded Q&A, and accurate citation.

- Supports multi-tenant operations to host multiple enterprise clients on a single backend while keeping data isolated.


## Platform Highlights

1. **Intelligent Deck Narration**
   - AI agents drive slide presentations, syncing audio narration and visuals based on customer questions.

2. **Enterprise Knowledge Engine**
   - Documents are ingested, parsed, and indexed; agents can answer questions referencing company-specific content.

3. **Evidence Spans**
   - Every response includes precise citations (PDF page, paragraph coordinates) to maintain trust.

4. **Omnichannel Voice Interfaces**
   - WebRTC rooms, SIP telephony, and playground demos reuse the same orchestration layer.


## Implementation Details

- **Data Isolation**: PostgreSQL Row Level Security enforces tenant separation.

- **Admin Console**: Payload CMS powers document uploads, AI configuration, and deck templates.

- **Voice Stack**: Combines ASR, TTS, retrieval, and LLM orchestration via FastAPI + Pydantic AI.


## Business Value

- Lets enterprises spin up AI sales reps without months of onboarding.

- Citation-first design meets compliance needs in regulated industries (construction, finance, education).
