# Voice bear: Hybrid Inference on Apple Silicon


## Goals

- Deliver offline speech recognition tailored to Taiwanese Mandarin, Taiwanese Hokkien, and code-switching scenarios.

- Combine CoreML (ANE) and GGML (Metal) to maximize throughput while keeping response time real-time (RTR < 1).


## Technical Highlights

1. **Hybrid Pipeline**
   - Converted the Breeze-ASR-25 encoder into CoreML FP16 models running on the Apple Neural Engine.
   - Quantized the decoder with GGML Q5_K, leveraging Metal BF16 and Flash Attention to sustain streaming inference.

2. **Whisper.cpp Integration**
   - Used the C-API with `no_state` / `full_with_state` flows plus per-inference state isolation to prevent KV-cache contamination.

3. **audio_ctx = 1500 Alignment**
   - Ensured each 30-second chunk maps to 1,500 encoder tokens, avoiding misalignment during decoding.

4. **Heterogeneous Resource Allocation**
   - Dedicated ANE to encoder workloads while Metal GPU handled decoder tasks, balancing thermals and utilization.


## User Impact

- Achieves reliable streaming for 15-second audio slices on an M2 Air while preserving privacy with a fully offline stack.

- Accent-aware tuning significantly improves accuracy on Taiwanese accents and mixed Chinese-English utterances.


## Key Learnings

- Built a reusable blueprint for hybrid inference that can be applied to other ASR or LLM workloads on Apple Silicon.
